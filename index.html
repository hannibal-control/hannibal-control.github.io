    Format
    VST3 (64-bit) and standalone app

    macOS
    In preparation (AU and VST3)

    28 factory chains, your own presets, per-module presets

    A/B compare, undo / redo, bypass, mono check, phase, dry / processed mix

    Vocal rider and auto level keep the input steady
    Controls
